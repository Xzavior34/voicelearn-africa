# Phone Test Script (10–15 minutes)

Follow this on your Samsung phone in Chrome. You don't need to look at any code — just speak the
sentences, watch what happens, and capture what's asked.

**Deployment URL:** `[FILL IN YOUR DEPLOYED URL HERE]`

---

### 1. Open it and grant mic access (1 min)

Open the URL above in Chrome, go to **Learn**, tap the microphone button.

- Allow microphone access when Chrome asks.
- 📸 Screenshot the permission prompt.

---

### 2. Say these sentences, one at a time, waiting for each response (8–10 min)

Speak naturally, at a normal pace. After each one, wait for the app to respond before moving to
the next.

| # | Say this | Watch for |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | "Why do plants need sunlight for photosynthesis?" | An explanation about chlorophyll/photosynthesis, plus a follow-up question. |
| 2 | "What is evaporation?" | The topic changes to evaporation. **It must NOT repeat the photosynthesis answer from #1.** |
| 3 | "Why water fit evaporate even when e never reach 100 degrees?" | Understood as an evaporation question, even mixing Pidgin and English. |
| 4 | "So what's the difference between evaporation and boiling?" | Stays on evaporation, gives a comparison — doesn't act like a brand-new topic. |
| 5 | "Why do clouds look white?" | Recognized as a new topic. Should say it's not something it currently knows, **not** answer with the evaporation content from #3/#4. |
| 6 | *(answer the tutor's next follow-up question correctly)* | Positive feedback, next question is different/harder. |
| 7 | *(answer a follow-up question incorrectly, on purpose)* | Explains what's wrong, doesn't just repeat itself. |

📸 **Screenshot or screen-record steps 2 and 5 especially** — those two are the most important
evidence for this submission.

---

### 3. One rapid-fire test (1 min)

Ask a question, and — before it finishes responding — immediately ask a completely different
one (tap fast, or interrupt and ask again right away).

- Watch for: only the **second** question's answer should end up on screen. If you see the first
  (older) answer show up after the second, or something garbled/duplicated, note it.

---

### 4. Refresh test (1 min)

Refresh the page mid-conversation.

- Watch for: the page reloads cleanly (not blank or broken), and the microphone still works
  without you having to re-grant permission.

---

### 5. What to send back

1. All screenshots/recordings from above (especially steps 2 and 5).
2. For each numbered step (1–7, plus the rapid-fire and refresh tests): did it match "Watch for,"
   or not? A quick "yes" / "no + what happened instead" per step is enough.
3. Your phone model, Android version, and Chrome version (Settings → About phone; Chrome →
   Settings → About Chrome).
4. Anything that felt broken, confusing, or slow, even if it's not in the list above.

This is genuinely the last missing piece of evidence for the submission — everything else has
already been verified through automated tests and a live (non-browser) check of the same API this
page calls.
